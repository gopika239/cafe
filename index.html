Our Story

More Than Coffee —
A Way of Life

Brewed & Co. was born in a narrow street in East London with one espresso machine, two mismatched chairs, and an obsession with extraordinary coffee. Sixteen years later, the obsession remains.

Every bean we source has a story — from the high-altitude farms of Ethiopia's Yirgacheffe to the volcanic slopes of Guatemala. We roast in small batches to honour those stories in every sip.
